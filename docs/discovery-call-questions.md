# Discovery Call — Phase 1 Question Bank

Reference material for the AI assessment's Discovery Call (Phase 1). Goal: pull out
bottlenecks and pain points without immediately prescribing solutions. Bite your
tongue on tools/solutions during this call — discovery is strictly for probing.

See [src/lib/discovery-script.ts](../src/lib/discovery-script.ts) for the scripted
version of this interview actually used by the app; this doc is the fuller source
question bank it draws from.

## 1. Understanding Business Context & Operations

Understand the "stack" and structure of the business before diving into AI.

- "What do you do in your business and how long have you been doing it?"
- "Do you run this solo or do you have a team helping you?"
- "What tools or software are you and the team using day-to-day?" (surfaces the current tech stack)

## 2. Identifying Daily Workflows & Friction

Find specific tasks that are candidates for AI efficiency.

- "Walk me through your day yesterday. What do you tend to do in a typical business day?"
- "What are tasks in your business that you dread doing?"
- "Where does your work pile up?" (identifies bottlenecks in the workflow)
- "What's the biggest headache in your workday right now?"

## 3. Probing for Automation Opportunities

See where the business owner has already identified a need for help.

- "What are things that you've tried to automate in the past and failed?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand and basically delete any process in your business, what would that process be?" (a common answer is email management)
- "Show me how you do [a specific task] today." — enables an AOA (Audit, Optimize, Automate) analysis: observe the steps so you can cut the "fat" before automating.

## 4. Defining Success & Urgency

Set clear expectations so the client sees the value in the assessment and any
future implementation.

- "90 days from now, what would make this engagement feel like a win?"
- "Of these recommendations, which is the most urgent for you?"
- "Are these pain points killing you, or is this something you could live with for another 60 days?"

## Strategic Advice for the Interview

- **Bite your tongue.** Avoid prescribing tools during the first call. The discovery
  phase is strictly for probing.
